How wall protection works & keyboard controls

Outline the wall to create a hard boundary, then click a bare wall area with Smart select. It follows similar connected pixels and stops at strong brightness or colour changes. Lower tolerance for tighter object protection; raise it gently to include shadows. Similar-coloured objects can still need Erase. This is edge-aware selection, not AI object recognition.

Erase keeps objects protected even after tolerance changes. Restore paint brings back erased areas only within the detected wall. Choose Add another wall for a separate colour. Undo restores up to 10 selection changes for the current wall.

Keyboard: focus the photo and move with arrow keys (Shift for fine movement). In Outline, Space adds a corner and Enter sets the boundary. In Smart select, Enter or Space selects the wall at the crosshair. Backspace undoes; Escape cancels an outline.
