Can I try colours on my own room?

Yes. Open Visualize, upload a JPG, PNG, or WebP photo, outline only the wall and choose Set wall boundary, then click bare wall with Smart select. Adjust edge tolerance or use Erase to protect objects, try a shade, and download the preview. Editing stays in your browser; saving while logged in stores a private copy in your account.

Can I change the colours in a pattern?

Absolutely. Open Patterns, choose a design, and select Customise this pattern. Change the background, pattern colour, style, and scale, then save your version.

Will the paint look exactly like the preview?

The photo preview is an impression of your idea. Lighting, screens, wall texture, and paint finish affect the final result. Try a physical paint sample before decorating.

Can other people use my pattern?

Yes. Saved patterns appear in the shared collection for people using this same app server. Log in to publish; your account name appears beside your design.
